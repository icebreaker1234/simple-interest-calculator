# Simple Interest Calculator

A small Bash command-line program that calculates simple interest from a principal amount, an annual rate of interest, and a time period in years.

## Formula

```text
Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100
```

The calculator displays the interest earned and the total amount (principal plus interest).

## Run

```bash
chmod +x simple-interest.sh
./simple-interest.sh
```

Enter the principal, annual interest rate (percent), and time period in years when prompted. For example, a principal of `1000`, rate of `5`, and time of `2` produces simple interest of `100.00` and a total amount of `1100.00`.
